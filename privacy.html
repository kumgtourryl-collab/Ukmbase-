Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. The types of data we collect include:

  • Account Information: Your email address, name, and authentication data (via GitHub or email/password).
  • Project Data: The schemas, tables, files, and records you create or store using Ukmbase Data.
  • AI Prompts: The text you input into UkmAI to generate schemas. This is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate the output.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the dashboard, sync operations, and payment logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Generate AI responses based on your prompts.
  • Process payments and track revenue via UkmPay.
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support messages.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including Row Level Security (RLS) on our database, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Ukmbase integrates with third-party services. By using Ukmbase, you acknowledge that your data may be processed by:

  • Supabase: For database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Groq: For AI inference processing.
  • Payment Providers: Stripe, Paystack, EcoCash, etc., for billing.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact support.
